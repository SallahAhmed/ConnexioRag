Design thinking is a human-centered approach to innovation and problem-solving that has been popularized by IDEO and Stanford's d.school. It is a methodology that provides a solution-based approach to solving complex problems by understanding human needs, challenging assumptions, redefining problems, and creating innovative solutions to prototype and test. Design thinking is particularly valuable for tackling ill-defined or wicked problems where the requirements are not fully understood.

The design thinking process consists of five phases that are iterative rather than linear. Empathize is the first phase where designers seek to understand the user's experience, motivations, and needs through observation, interviews, and immersion. This phase requires setting aside assumptions and gathering real insights about who the users are and what matters to them. Define is where the team synthesizes their findings from the empathy phase into a clear problem statement. A well-crafted problem statement is human-centered, broad enough to allow creative solutions, and narrow enough to be actionable. The define phase often produces a point of view statement that reframes the problem based on user insights.

Ideate is the divergent thinking phase where the team generates a wide range of possible solutions without judgment. Techniques include brainstorming, brainwriting, worst possible idea, and SCAMPER. The goal is quantity over quality at this stage. Prototype is where ideas are made tangible through low-fidelity representations like paper sketches, storyboards, wireframes, or role-playing. Prototypes are created quickly and cheaply to test specific aspects of a solution. Test is where prototypes are shared with users to gather feedback and learn what works and what doesn't. Testing often reveals new insights that send the team back to earlier phases. The iterative nature of design thinking means that teams cycle through these phases multiple times, each time refining their understanding and solution based on real feedback from users.
