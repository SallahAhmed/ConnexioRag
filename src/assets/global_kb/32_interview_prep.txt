Technical interview preparation requires practice across multiple skill areas. Technology companies typically assess candidates through a combination of coding problems, system design discussions, behavioral questions, and portfolio reviews. Understanding each component and preparing systematically significantly improves your chances of success.

Coding interviews test your ability to solve algorithmic problems efficiently. Common topics include arrays and strings manipulation, linked lists, trees and graphs, hash tables, dynamic programming, recursion, sorting and searching algorithms. Platforms like LeetCode, HackerRank, and Codewars provide extensive practice problems organized by difficulty and topic. Effective preparation involves solving problems consistently, studying common patterns, practicing writing code by hand or on a whiteboard, explaining your thought process while solving, and analyzing time and space complexity. Many candidates find the Blind 75 or NeetCode 150 problem lists useful for covering the most important patterns.

System design interviews assess your ability to architect scalable systems. Topics include database design, API architecture, caching strategies, load balancing, microservices, message queues, CDNs, and cloud services. Resources like System Design Interview by Alex Xu and Grokking the System Design Interview provide frameworks for approaching these questions. Behavioral interviews explore your past experiences using the STAR method, Situation, Task, Action, and Result. Prepare stories that demonstrate teamwork, leadership, conflict resolution, technical decision-making, failure and learning, and initiative. Common questions include tell me about a time you had a disagreement with a teammate, describe a project you led, and what is your biggest failure and what did you learn from it. Portfolio discussions let you showcase your projects, explaining your technical choices, challenges faced, and impact delivered. Practice talking through your code, design decisions, and tradeoffs confidently.
