To get started on Connexio, users register with their email or Google account through the authentication system. The platform supports OAuth-based login which creates a new user account or authenticates an existing one. After successful registration, users receive a JWT token that is used for all subsequent API requests to authenticate their identity and authorize their actions within the platform.

After registration, users complete their profile to maximize the platform's value. A complete profile includes FullName, a professional photo, a bio describing their background and interests, field of experience (such as software development, design, or marketing), technologies and skills listed as comma-separated values, a rating score based on past project performance, experience level (beginner, intermediate, or advanced), and years of experience. Users can also add their LinkedIn and GitHub profile links to showcase their professional work.

A well-completed profile is essential because the MasarX Agent's team matching algorithm uses this information to calculate compatibility scores with available projects. The algorithm weighs skills complementarity at 35%, availability at 25%, past ratings at 20%, phase experience at 12%, learning goals alignment at 5%, and domain alignment at 3%. Users with complete profiles receive better-matched project recommendations and are more likely to be invited to join teams. Users can update their profile at any time as their skills and experience grow.
