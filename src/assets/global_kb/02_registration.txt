To get started on Connexio, users register with their email or Google account through the authentication system. The platform supports OAuth-based login which creates a new user account or authenticates an existing one. After successful registration, users receive a secure token that is used for all subsequent API requests to authenticate their identity and authorize their actions within the platform.

After registration, users complete their profile to maximize the platform's value. A complete profile includes FullName, a professional photo, a bio describing their background and interests, field of experience (such as software development, design, or marketing), technologies and skills listed as comma-separated values, a rating score based on past project performance, experience level (beginner, intermediate, or advanced), and years of experience. Users can also add their LinkedIn and GitHub profile links to showcase their professional work.

A well-completed profile is essential because the team matching system uses this information to calculate compatibility scores with available projects. The algorithm considers skills, availability, past ratings, experience, learning goals, and domain alignment. Users with complete profiles receive better-matched project recommendations and are more likely to be invited to join teams. Users can update their profile at any time as their skills and experience grow.
