The Business Model Canvas is a strategic management template developed by Alexander Osterwalder for developing new business models or documenting existing ones. It provides a visual chart with nine building blocks that describe a company's value proposition, infrastructure, customers, and finances. The canvas is designed to be concise, visual, and intuitive, fitting on a single page, which makes it easy to discuss, analyze, and iterate.

The nine building blocks are organized into four main areas. Customer side includes Customer Segments which define the different groups of people or organizations the business aims to reach and serve. Value Proposition describes the bundle of products and services that create value for each customer segment, solving their problems or satisfying their needs. Channels cover how the company communicates with and reaches its customer segments to deliver the value proposition. Customer Relationships describe the types of relationships the company establishes with specific customer segments, from personal assistance to automated self-service. Revenue Streams represent the cash the company generates from each customer segment through sales, subscriptions, licensing, or other mechanisms.

Business side includes Key Resources which are the most important assets required to make the business model work, including physical, intellectual, human, and financial resources. Key Activities are the most important actions the company must perform to deliver its value proposition, such as production, problem-solving, or platform/network management. Key Partnerships describe the network of suppliers and partners that help the company operate, including strategic alliances and buyer-supplier relationships. Cost Structure describes all costs incurred to operate the business model, including fixed costs, variable costs, economies of scale, and economies of scope. The canvas is used by entrepreneurs and intrapreneurs to brainstorm business ideas, analyze existing businesses, identify opportunities for innovation, and communicate business models to stakeholders. It is often used alongside the Value Proposition Canvas which provides deeper detail on the value proposition building block.
