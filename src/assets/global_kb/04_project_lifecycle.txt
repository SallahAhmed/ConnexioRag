Projects on Connexio follow a structured lifecycle with clearly defined phases that guide teams from ideation to completion. The lifecycle is designed to mirror real-world software development practices while providing enough structure to keep teams on track and accountable. Each phase has specific deliverables that must be completed before the team can advance to the next stage.

The first phase is ideation and team formation where the project owner creates the project by defining its name, description, goals, scope, and required technologies. The owner then recruits team members either by inviting specific users or by opening the project to applications that the matching algorithm evaluates. Once the team is assembled, the planning and design phase begins where the team creates wireframes, system architecture documents, user stories, and a work breakdown structure that decomposes the project into manageable tasks. The MasarX Agent can assist by generating initial task lists and milestone plans.

The development phase follows with sprint-based execution using agile methodologies. Teams work in iterations, typically one to two weeks long, with regular check-ins and progress tracking. After development comes testing and quality assurance where the team validates functionality, fixes bugs, and ensures the product meets requirements. The final phase is deployment and retrospective where the team launches the project and conducts a retrospective meeting to reflect on what went well, what could be improved, and what lessons were learned. The MasarX Agent generates retrospective documents and milestone reports automatically. Phase transitions are gated by deliverable completion and team readiness validation.
