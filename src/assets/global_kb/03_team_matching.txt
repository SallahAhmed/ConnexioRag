Connexio matches team members to projects using a comprehensive compatibility evaluation that considers multiple factors including technical skills, availability, experience, and project requirements. The system reviews a user's skills, past project performance, and learning objectives to suggest well-matched project opportunities. This helps ensure that teams have the right mix of expertise to succeed.

When a project needs team members, the system identifies users whose profiles align with the project's needs and presents recommendations to project owners. Each recommendation includes an explanation of why the user would be a good fit, highlighting their relevant skills and experience. Users can browse available projects that match their profile and apply to join.

The matching process is designed to create balanced, effective teams by considering both what the project needs and what each team member brings to the table. Team composition can evolve over time as projects progress through different phases and new needs arise.
